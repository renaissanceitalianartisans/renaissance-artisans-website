Why clients choose Renaissance.

A kitchen touches every part of a home, so it shouldn't be split among a designer, a showroom, a workshop, and an installer who have never met. Renaissance is based in both Florence and Miami, so one company takes care of all of it. We design your kitchen, our team in Florence makes every piece specifically for your home, we bring it to Miami, and our professional installation team puts it in place. You always know who to call, and the kitchen you approved on paper is exactly the kitchen you get.

We also take on only a few homes at a time. Your kitchen has our full attention from the first conversation to the final installation, and it comes together in remarkable time.

A kitchen is often where a larger project begins. Many of our clients pair theirs with closets, a staircase, or a complete interior renovation, so every room shares the same level of craft.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We meet to understand your home and what you want your kitchen to be.

  2. 2

    Design

    We visit your home to take precise measurements, then our in-house design team develops your kitchen, or we work from your architect's or designer's plans. You approve every drawing before anything is made. The design fee is credited toward your kitchen once you move forward with us.

  3. 3

    Made in Italy

    Our team in Florence makes every piece to order with exceptional care, then assembles the complete kitchen in our workshop to confirm each detail before it leaves Italy. Depending on the scope of your project, this stage takes an average of four to six weeks.

  4. 4

    Shipping to Miami

    We arrange every part of the journey from our workshop in Florence to your home. By cargo ship, your kitchen arrives inside your home in about four weeks, ready for installation to begin. By cargo plane, it can be there within one week.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our professional installation team installs your kitchen and walks through the finished space with you.
