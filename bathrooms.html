Bathrooms.

A bathroom should feel like a retreat you never have to leave home for.

We design luxury bathrooms in Miami with the same care as every other room in your home. Every piece is custom made by our team in Florence and installed by our installation team in Miami.

Clients come to us when they…

  • Want a primary bath that feels like a private spa.
  • Want every bathroom in the house finished to the same standard, guest baths included.
  • Want the feeling of a beautiful hotel suite, every day, at home.
  • Want a vanity and storage designed around the way they get ready.

How we design your bathroom.

Every bathroom begins with a conversation about how you want it to feel, from the calm of a morning routine to the ease of an evening bath. Our in-house design team creates each vanity, cabinet, and storage piece from a blank page, or we build from the plans of your own architect or designer. Because every piece is custom made, it fits your space exactly.

Why clients choose Renaissance.

A bathroom is a small room with a great many details: how a drawer opens, where the light falls, how one surface meets the next. Each of them deserves attention. Because we take on only a few homes at a time, your bathroom receives our full attention from the first drawing to the final installation, and it comes together in remarkable time.

Renaissance is based in both Florence and Miami, so one company designs, makes, ships, and installs every piece, and you always know who to call.

A bathroom is rarely the only room that deserves this attention. Many of our clients pair theirs with closets, a kitchen, or a complete interior renovation, so every room is finished to the same standard.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We meet to understand your home and what you want your bathroom to be.

  2. 2

    Design

    We visit your home to take precise measurements, then our in-house design team develops your bathroom, or we work from your architect's or designer's plans. You approve every drawing before anything is made. The design fee is credited toward your bathroom once you move forward with us.

  3. 3

    Made in Italy

    Our team in Florence makes every piece to order with exceptional care and checks each detail in our workshop before it leaves Italy. Depending on the scope of your project, this stage takes an average of four to six weeks.

  4. 4

    Shipping to Miami

    We arrange every part of the journey from our workshop in Florence to your home. By cargo ship, your bathroom pieces arrive inside your home in about four weeks, ready for installation to begin. By cargo plane, they can be there within one week.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our professional installation team installs every piece and walks through the finished space with you.
