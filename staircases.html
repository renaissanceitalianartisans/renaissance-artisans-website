How we design your staircase.

Every staircase begins with a conversation about your home and how you want it to feel as you move through it. Our in-house design team creates the staircase from a blank page, or we build from the plans of your own architect or designer. Because every piece is custom made, your staircase is shaped precisely to your space, from the first step to the last.

Why clients choose Renaissance.

A staircase is built into the structure of the house itself, so every measurement has to be exact. When the design, the making, and the installation are split among different companies, small differences add up. Renaissance is based in both Florence and Miami, so one company takes care of all of it. We design your staircase, our team in Florence makes every piece specifically for your home, we bring it to Miami, and our professional installation team sets it in place, so every detail fits exactly as drawn.

We take on only a few homes at a time, so your staircase has our full attention and comes together in remarkable time.

Because a staircase connects every floor, it is often the starting point for something larger. Many of our clients pair theirs with a kitchen, closets, or a complete interior renovation, so the whole home carries the same level of craft.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We meet to understand your home and what you want your staircase to be.

  2. 2

    Design

    We visit your home to take precise measurements, then our in-house design team develops your staircase, or we work from your architect's or designer's plans. You approve every drawing before anything is made. The design fee is credited toward your staircase once you move forward with us.

  3. 3

    Made in Italy

    Our team in Florence makes every piece to order with exceptional care, then assembles the complete staircase in our workshop to confirm each detail before it leaves Italy. Depending on the scope of your project, this stage takes an average of four to six weeks.

  4. 4

    Shipping to Miami

    We arrange every part of the journey from our workshop in Florence to your home. By cargo ship, your staircase arrives inside your home in about four weeks, ready for installation to begin. By cargo plane, it can be there within one week.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our professional installation team installs your staircase and walks through the finished space with you.
