Why clients choose Renaissance.

Most luxury interiors are pieced together: a designer in one place, a showroom in another, a workshop overseas, and an installer who has never seen the drawings. Renaissance was built to work differently. We are based in both Florence and Miami, so one company designs every piece, our team in Florence makes it specifically for your home, and our professional installation team puts it in place. You work with us directly, from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.

Every piece we create is designed for a single home, to a standard of quality you can see in the finish and feel each time you open a drawer or walk up a stair. And because we take on only a few projects at a time, each one receives our full attention and comes together in remarkable time.

From Florence to your home.

It begins with a private consultation. We then measure your space and design every piece, or work from your architect's or designer's plans. Our team in Florence makes each piece to order, we ship it to Miami by sea in about four weeks or by air within one week, and our professional installation team completes the installation.

A family company with roots in Florence.

Renaissance is a family company with roots in Florence, built on the belief that a home should be made with exceptional care. We named the company after the city where our story began, and we bring that same spirit to every home we work in.
