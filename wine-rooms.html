Design Inspiration

How we design your wine room.

Every wine room begins with a conversation about your collection and how you want to enjoy it, whether that means quiet evenings or tastings with guests. Our in-house design team plans the racking, the display, and the space around them from a blank page, or we build from the plans of your own architect or designer. Because every piece is custom made, your wine room is shaped exactly to your space and your collection.

Why clients choose Renaissance.

A wine room brings many elements together in one space: the racking, the display, the lighting, and the room around them. With Renaissance, one company takes care of all of it. Renaissance is based in both Florence and Miami, so we design your wine room, our team in Florence makes every piece specifically for your home, we bring it to Miami, and our professional installation team puts it in place. You always know who to call.

We take on only a few homes at a time, so your wine room has our full attention and comes together in remarkable time.

A wine room is often part of a larger project, planned alongside a kitchen, a cinema room, or a complete interior renovation, so the whole home shares the same level of craft.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We meet to understand your home, your collection, and what you want your wine room to be.

  2. 2

    Design

    We visit your home to take precise measurements, then our in-house design team develops your wine room, or we work from your architect's or designer's plans. You approve every drawing before anything is made. The design fee is credited toward your wine room once you move forward with us.

  3. 3

    Made in Italy

    Our team in Florence makes every piece to order with exceptional care and checks each detail in our workshop before it leaves Italy. Depending on the scope of your project, this stage takes an average of four to six weeks.

  4. 4

    Shipping to Miami

    We arrange every part of the journey from our workshop in Florence to your home. By cargo ship, your wine room arrives inside your home in about four weeks, ready for installation to begin. By cargo plane, it can be there within one week.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our professional installation team installs your wine room and walks through the finished space with you.
