Design Inspiration

How we design your cinema room.

Every cinema room begins with a conversation about how you want to use it, from family movie nights to evenings with guests. Our in-house design team plans the wall paneling, the seating platforms, and the cabinetry around your screen from a blank page, or we build from the plans of your own architect or designer. Because every piece is custom made, the room is shaped exactly to your space.

Why clients choose Renaissance.

A cinema room only feels right when every element works together: the walls, the seating, the cabinetry, and the way the light falls. With Renaissance, one company takes care of all of it. Renaissance is based in both Florence and Miami, so we design your cinema room, our team in Florence makes every piece specifically for your home, we bring it to Miami, and our professional installation team puts it in place. You always know who to call.

We take on only a few homes at a time, so your cinema room has our full attention and comes together in remarkable time.

A cinema room is often part of a larger project, planned alongside a wine room or a complete interior renovation, so evenings with guests flow naturally from one room to the next.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We meet to understand your home and what you want your cinema room to be.

  2. 2

    Design

    We visit your home to take precise measurements, then our in-house design team develops your cinema room, or we work from your architect's or designer's plans. You approve every drawing before anything is made. The design fee is credited toward your cinema room once you move forward with us.

  3. 3

    Made in Italy

    Our team in Florence makes every piece to order with exceptional care and checks each detail in our workshop before it leaves Italy. Depending on the scope of your project, this stage takes an average of four to six weeks.

  4. 4

    Shipping to Miami

    We arrange every part of the journey from our workshop in Florence to your home. By cargo ship, your cinema room arrives inside your home in about four weeks, ready for installation to begin. By cargo plane, it can be there within one week.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our professional installation team installs your cinema room and walks through the finished space with you.
