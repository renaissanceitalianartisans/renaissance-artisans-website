Our story.

Renaissance was founded by the Chamula family, Fernando and his sons Diego and David, who lived in Italy for several years before making Miami their home. In Italy, they grew used to a level of craftsmanship that shaped how they saw every room: the care in a finish, the way each piece was made for the space it lived in. In Miami, they found that standard hard to come by.

So they brought it with them. Today Renaissance is based in both Florence and Miami. Our team in Florence makes every piece to order, and our professional installation team in Miami puts it in place.

Why we're called Renaissance.

Our name is an homage to our family's roots in Florence, the city where the Renaissance began. It is a reminder of where our craft comes from, and of the standard we hold every home to.

How we work.

We take on large-scale projects only, and only a few at a time, so every home receives our full attention. Some clients come to us with plans from their own architect or designer, and we build from those drawings exactly as specified. Others come without a design in mind, and our in-house design team creates one with them.

Either way, one company takes care of everything, from the first conversation to the final walkthrough, and your project comes together in remarkable time. We work with homeowners, architects, designers, developers, and retail brands across Miami.
