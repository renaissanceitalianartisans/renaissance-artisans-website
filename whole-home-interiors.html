Clients come to us when they…

  • Want every room in their home to share the same elegance and level of craft.
  • Are renovating a new home and want it finished all at once, every room in harmony.
  • Want to walk into a home that feels entirely their own, from the entrance to the last room.
  • Want one team responsible for the whole interior, start to finish.

How we design your home.

Every renovation begins with a conversation about your home as a whole: how you live in it, how you host in it, and how you want it to feel from room to room. Our in-house design team plans the complete interior from a blank page, or we build from the plans of your own architect or designer. Because every piece is custom made, each room connects naturally to the next.

For projects that call for construction work, we work with our trusted building partner, or with your own builder.

Why clients choose Renaissance.

When a whole home is being renovated, the hardest part is often keeping every company, schedule, and decision in step. With Renaissance, one company takes care of the entire interior. Renaissance is based in both Florence and Miami, so we design every room, our team in Florence makes every piece specifically for your home, we bring it to Miami, and our professional installation team puts it all in place. You have one point of contact throughout, and every room is finished to the same standard.

We take on only a few homes at a time, so your renovation has our full attention and comes together in remarkable time.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We meet to understand your home and what you want it to become.

  2. 2

    Design

    We visit your home to take precise measurements, then our in-house design team develops the complete interior, or we work from your architect's or designer's plans. You approve every drawing before anything is made. The design fee is credited toward your renovation once you move forward with us. Once the design is complete, we can give you an estimate of your project's timeline.

  3. 3

    Made in Italy

    Our team in Florence makes every piece to order with exceptional care and checks each detail in our workshop before it leaves Italy.

  4. 4

    Shipping to Miami

    We arrange every part of the journey from our workshop in Florence to your home, including export paperwork, insured shipping, and U.S. customs clearance. Your pieces are delivered directly to your home, ready for installation to begin.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our professional installation team installs every piece and walks through the finished home with you. We also coordinate the permits your project requires.
