Luxury Retail.

A store should feel as considered as what it sells.

We create luxury retail interiors in Miami for flagship stores and boutiques in the city's most prestigious shopping destinations, including the Design District, Bal Harbour, Key Biscayne, Merrick Park, Coconut Grove, and Coral Gables. Every piece is custom made by our team in Florence and installed by our installation team in Miami.

Brands come to us when they…

  • Are opening a flagship or boutique in Miami and want it to stand apart.
  • Want their store to reflect the same standard as their brand around the world.
  • Want display pieces and fixtures designed for their collection, not chosen from a standard line.
  • Have a firm opening date and need a partner who will meet it.

How we design your store.

Every store begins with a conversation about your brand, your collection, and the experience you want each client to have from the moment they walk in. Our in-house design team creates the interior from a blank page, or we build from the plans of your architect, designer, or brand team. Because every piece is custom made, your store is shaped exactly to your space and your collection.

Why brands choose Renaissance.

A store opening runs on a fixed date, and every piece has to be in place before the doors open. Because we take on only a few projects at a time, your store has our full attention, and we plan our work around your opening.

Renaissance is based in both Florence and Miami, so one company designs, makes, ships, and installs every piece, and you have one point of contact throughout.

Many brands bring us the entire store rather than a single piece, so every part of the space feels like one design, from the entrance to the last display.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We meet to understand your brand and what you want your store to be.

  2. 2

    Design

    We visit your space to take precise measurements, then our in-house design team develops your store, or we work from your architect's, designer's, or brand team's plans. You approve every drawing before anything is made. The design fee is credited toward your project once you move forward with us.

  3. 3

    Made in Italy

    Our team in Florence makes every piece to order with exceptional care and checks each detail in our workshop before it leaves Italy. Depending on the scope of your project, this stage takes an average of four to six weeks.

  4. 4

    Shipping to Miami

    We arrange every part of the journey from our workshop in Florence to your store. By cargo ship, your pieces arrive inside your store in about four weeks, ready for installation to begin. By cargo plane, they can be there within one week.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our professional installation team installs every piece on a schedule built around your opening and walks through the finished store with you.
