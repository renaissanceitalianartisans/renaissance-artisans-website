Frequently asked questions.

Where do you work?

We work exclusively in Miami.

What size projects do you take on?

Many of our clients begin with a single room, such as a kitchen or a staircase, and continue into the rest of the home. Most of our projects bring several rooms together, up to a complete interior renovation. We take on only a few at a time, so every home receives our full attention.

What kind of homes do you work on?

Our clients own some of the most exceptional homes in Miami, and they expect every piece to match the quality of the home around it.

Can you work with our architect or designer?

Yes. We build from your architect's or designer's plans exactly as specified, or our in-house design team can create the design with you.

What areas of craft do you offer?

Kitchens, closets, staircases, teak flooring, bathrooms, complete interior renovations, wine rooms, cinema rooms, and luxury retail spaces. Every piece is designed by us, custom made by our team in Florence, and installed by our professional installation team in Miami.

How long does a project take?

Depending on the scope of your project, our team in Florence completes the pieces in an average of four to six weeks. Shipping to Miami takes about four weeks by cargo ship, or within one week by cargo plane, and our professional installation team takes it from there.

How do I start a project?

Send us the form on this page or call us at (305) 414-1809, and we'll follow up to arrange a private consultation. We begin by understanding your home and the scope of your project before any design work starts.
