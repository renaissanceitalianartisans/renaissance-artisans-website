How we design your closet.

Every closet begins with a conversation about what you'd like it to hold and how you'd like it to feel. Our in-house design team plans every shelf, drawer, and display around your wishes, or we build from the plans of your own architect or designer. Because every piece is custom made, your closet is made exactly the size you want, down to each drawer and shelf.

Why clients choose Renaissance.

A closet is the room you use every single day, so you feel its quality each morning: in the way a drawer closes, a door settles into place, and the lighting greets you when you walk in. Our team in Florence makes every piece to order with exceptional care, to a standard you can see and feel.

Renaissance is based in both Florence and Miami, so one company designs, makes, ships, and installs your closet, and you always know who to call. We take on only a few homes at a time, so your closet has our full attention and comes together in remarkable time.

Many of our clients begin with a closet and continue into the rest of the home, pairing it with bathrooms, a kitchen, or a complete interior renovation, so every room feels connected.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We meet to understand your home and what you want your closet to be.

  2. 2

    Design

    We visit your home to take precise measurements, then our in-house design team develops your closet, or we work from your architect's or designer's plans. You approve every drawing before anything is made. The design fee is credited toward your closet once you move forward with us.

  3. 3

    Made in Italy

    Our team in Florence makes every piece to order with exceptional care, then assembles the complete closet in our workshop to confirm each detail before it leaves Italy. Depending on the scope of your project, this stage takes an average of four to six weeks.

  4. 4

    Shipping to Miami

    We arrange every part of the journey from our workshop in Florence to your home. By cargo ship, your closet arrives inside your home in about four weeks, ready for installation to begin. By cargo plane, it can be there within one week.

  5. 5

    Installation

    Our professional installation team installs your closet and walks through the finished space with you.
